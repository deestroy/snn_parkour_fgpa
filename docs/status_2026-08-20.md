## Year two (simulation-in-the-loop) — started early

The physical robot was dropped (2026-08-20); year two puts the real FPGA
inside a simulated quadruped control loop. Platform: the ES-Parkour
recreation (MuJoCo, trained teacher at 94–100 % success L3). Progress in
the first 24 h of year-two work:

- **P1 (the FPGA-sized network): done as a risk question.** A 58k-parameter
  encoder with the hardware's exact neuron semantics, distilled 96k
  iterations from the trained teacher, EQUALS OR BEATS the 11.19M-parameter
  reference student at 1/200th the size (step: 25 % vs 19 % at L3, 19 % vs
  0 % at L6; theoretical encoder energy 31x lower). Absolute success is low
  for BOTH students — the recipe, not encoder capacity, is the limiter
  (recorded as a finding).
- **The full checkpoint-to-silicon pipeline already works:** real simulated
  event frames -> int8/power-of-two quantisation (threshold 2^4) -> BOTH
  engines bit-identical at the 2x64x64 robot geometry (1M+ comparisons)
  -> 3.065 ms/inference (ED K=4) at the real 6 % activity — verified on
  the FINAL checkpoint (1M+ comparisons per engine); synthesis-ready baked
  weight modules regenerated from it.
- **S0 (sim reproducibility): PASS** — same seed, bit-identical rollouts.
- **S1 (deadline integrity): PASS** — in a simulator physics waits, so a
  naive loop can never miss a deadline; the perception-budget loop charges
  measured wall-clock time against an explicit budget and delivers STALE
  latents on misses, proven by injected delays. This protects the year-two
  experiment the way the gantry protected the robot.
