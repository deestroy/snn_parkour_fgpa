# Verified references for thesis Chapters 2-3

Generated 2026-09-20 by checking every work named informally in the
Chapter 2, Chapter 3 and "References" entries of `docs/thesis_outline_build.py`
against publisher pages, Crossref/DataCite records, arXiv and the papers
themselves. Rules used:

- **VERIFIED** — a real work exists and the informal name (author / venue / year) matches it.
- **CORRECTED** — a real work exists but the informal name has a wrong author, venue or year; the correction is given.
- **NOT FOUND** — no matching work could be located (none in this pass).
- DOIs are given only where a Crossref/DataCite record or the publisher page confirmed them. Blank means unconfirmed, not absent.
- Author lists are truncated to the first author plus up to three more, then "et al.".
- Nothing here was recalled from memory alone: every row was checked against at least one online record during this session.

Companion file: `docs/references.bib` (one BibTeX entry per VERIFIED or CORRECTED row, key = firstauthorYEARkeyword).
