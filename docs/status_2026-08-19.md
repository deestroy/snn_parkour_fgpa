At the trained C1 activity the event-driven engine is ~2.9x faster and
uses ~4x the block RAM and ~1.7x the logic. Energy is the open question and
is exactly what the meter answers.

## What it took to get the event-driven engine sign-off clean (thesis material)

Between "bit-identical in simulation" and "timing-clean on the fabric" five
things had to change, none of which the dense engine needed: BRAM port
discipline (a 62k-LUT overrun), no multipliers on address paths, no
dividers on address paths (addresses carried as fields, D0020 rev 2),
re-registered BRAM reads before the LIF arithmetic, and a Vivado refresh
that silently dropped the DMA address map. All logged with the timing
reports. This asymmetry is part of the "event-driven machinery costs
something" argument, with numbers.
