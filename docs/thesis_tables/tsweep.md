# Timesteps T vs accuracy and the int16 membrane ceiling

Generated by make_tables.py from the golden-check logs per (dataset, T, seed). Accuracy mean +- sd over seeds (sample sd); fc membrane use = peak |V| as a fraction of int16 over the golden run; a network 'overflows' when that exceeds 100 %% (the hardware membrane is int16, the golden model's is int32 -- see C0046). N-MNIST T=4 has no golden log with a membrane line in the repository; its float accuracy comes from the M0 CSV.

**Why DVS-Gesture at T = 8 and T = 16 shows two seeds and not three.** Three
seeds were run for each. Seed 0's original golden-check logs were overwritten
on 2026-09-20 before anyone could see that they were not tracked (C0051), so
this generator can no longer read them. Their numbers survive, transcribed at
the time, in `experiments/dvsgesture/README.md`: T = 8 seed 0 fc -28,768 ..
22,533 (88 % of int16, fits); T = 16 seed 0 fc -34,472 .. 28,983 (105 %,
overflows), float 68.94 %, golden 70.83 %. Including them, T = 16 overflows on
two seeds of three and T = 8 on one of three, which is what the READMEs and the
ledger state. A retrain of seed 0 is in `experiments/dvsgesture/c0046/
rerun_seed0/` but is deliberately NOT counted here: it is a different network,
because training does not reproduce at a fixed seed (C0050).
