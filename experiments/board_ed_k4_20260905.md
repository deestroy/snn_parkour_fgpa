# Board pass: ED K=4 with pipelined sweep + word-parallel wrapper (2026-09-05)

Build: ENGINE=1, ED_K=4, BAKED_WEIGHTS=1, N_ENGINES=1, WNS +0.508, 100 MHz.
Server: build 2 (build 3 ELF not yet in BOOT.bin — sweep/XADC pending).
Pre-write checks all green (.hwh params, MT41J128M16, HP0, 3 MEMRANGEs
incl. both DMA data ranges, BOOT.bin PL partition byte-equal to .xsa).

## Correctness

16/16 N-MNIST check samples bit-identical to the golden model (9,280
output words). First silicon run of the C0030 pipelined sweep, the C0035
word-parallel wrapper, and the baked ed_scatter_c1 — all validated.

## Against prediction and prior baselines

- Simulation predicted ~678 us (67,756 cycles) on the seeded trace set.
  Board mean is 723.5 us on the N-MNIST check set: +6.7%, consistent
  with per-pass DMA/driver overhead plus the different sample set.
  Sample 12 happens to sit exactly at 678.9 us.
- Old ED build (bit-serial wrapper, unpipelined sweep): 1.51 ms
  -> **2.1x faster** from C0030 + C0035 combined, on silicon.
- Dense P=1 board (functional-only pass): 4.409 ms -> 6.1x vs mean.
  The matched-parallelism comparison needs Build 2 (dense P=4,
  predicted ~1.04 ms); until then 1.54x remains a simulation number.

Mean **688.5 us**, range 554.8-815.8
(1.47x data-dependent spread). Server overhead constant at 117.5-117.6 us
per pass, reported separately. Internal consistency: the 16-sample sweep's
engine mean (688.5) equals the mean of the 16 single-sample runs (688.45).

- **vs sim prediction 678 us: +1.5 %** — the earlier "+6.7 % DMA overhead"
  was mostly the server's CRC loop; the true engine+DMA cost is within
  measurement noise of the cycle model.
- vs old ED build 1.51 ms: **2.2x** (engine-only).

Also verified on silicon (build 4): BURST sweep mode across all 16 loaded
samples (C0018) with per-sample determinism CRCs; XADC die temperatures in
the reply (C0009), 35.0-39.6 degC across the session; and the sweep's
ring-rotation fix (a lone extra load between invocations rotated slot
order away from load order — build 3 failed the client CRC check exactly
then; build 4 sweeps from the oldest slot and passed with the ring
deliberately offset by one).
