## Against the pre-registration

| prediction | predicted | measured | held |
|---|---|---|---|
| correctness | 8/8 | 8/8 | yes |
| per-clip ordering | clip 0 fastest, clip 1 slowest | same | yes |
| spread | 2.30x | 2.24x | yes |
| board = sim + offset | offset ~38 us if DMA streaming, ~10 us if fixed | **+92.9 us, constant to +-0.8 us on every clip** | **neither** |
| ED wins | 6 of 8 | 6 of 8 (clips 1 and 5 above dense) | yes, pending dense P=4 silicon |

Fit across the 8 clips: board = 0.9998 x sim + 93.5 us, residual within
0.8 us. So the cycle model transfers to the second dataset exactly, up
to a per-pass constant -- but the constant is 92.9 us, not the 30-38 us
the streaming hypothesis gave, nor the ~10 us of a fixed cost. Two
things are known to differ from the N-MNIST comparison and must be
separated before the offset is interpreted: (a) the g1 sim column is
"first spike push -> done" per timestep, which EXCLUDES the wrapper's
input-word streaming, whereas the N-MNIST ksweep "total" column
included the wrapper (~1.9k cycles); (b) the frames are 3.5x larger
(1,024 in / 2,048 out words), so DMA streaming, the server's cache
maintenance on the buffers, and the wrapper's word unpacking all grow.
The dense P=4 pass gives a second offset on the same frames; the
harness can produce a wrapper-inclusive cycle count. Until then the
offset is recorded as measured, not explained.

## What this settles

- ED at K=4 on DVS-Gesture: bit-identical on silicon; per-clip latency
  is the simulated cycle count plus one constant.
- The activity crossover INSIDE a dataset, predicted by the sims (the
  two densest clips lose to dense P=4), is visible on the ED side:
  clips 1 and 5 sit above the dense prediction. The verdict needs the
  dense build on the same board (Build B).

## Offset resolved (2026-09-19, 23:55): the comparison basis, not the hardware

The g1 per-engine sim column ("first spike push -> done") excludes the
AXIS wrapper's word streaming. Re-run through the baked AXIS harness
with its wrapper-inclusive "total" column (NOGAP=1 CYCLES=..., the
basis the N-MNIST ksweep used; files in latency_sim/axis_total/):

| engine | wrapper cycles / inference | board - wrapper-inclusive sim |
|---|---|---|
| ED K=4 | 6,248-6,516 (per clip) | **29.4 us, constant to +-0.7 us** (28.7-30.1) |
| dense P=4 | 8,195 | **20.0 us** |

So ~63 us (ED) and ~82 us (dense) of the apparent 92.9 / 102.0 us were
wrapper streaming that the simulation column simply did not count. The
remaining per-pass offsets, with N-MNIST's on the same basis
(872 words: ED 10.7, dense 8.3), fit a fixed ~3.5 us plus ~8.5 ns/word
(ED) or ~5.3 ns/word (dense): DMA and cache maintenance scaling with
the frame, plus a fixed setup -- the streaming hypothesis in kind. The
2026-09-19 "neither hypothesis held" lines above stand as written (they
were written against the wrong basis); this section supersedes them.
